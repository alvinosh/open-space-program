Support the game.

Open Space Program is free to download and play. If you like it and want to help it along, you can chip in on Ko-fi. Donating does not unlock anything in the game.

It is early and unfinished. What is planned is on the roadmap.

Ways to help

Report a bug
Open an issue on GitHub with what you did and what happened. A screenshot helps.
Make a mod
Planets, parts, craft and scenarios are records that mods can add or patch. The modding guide explains how.
Spread the word
Tell someone who likes spaceflight, or star the repo.
