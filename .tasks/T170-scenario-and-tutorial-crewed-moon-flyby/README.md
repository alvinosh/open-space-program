# T170: Scenario and tutorial: crewed Moon flyby

A crew goes round the Moon and comes home. The first crewed Moon scenario, and the one that makes the entry, the heat shield, the parachutes and the sea worth building. The landing (T171) builds on it.

## Scope

- Craft: a Moon stack launched from the Cape (start from `colossus`), with a crew capsule that has a heat shield and parachutes. Hand-built, flown until it is fun.
- Goals: orbit, translunar injection, a flyby under 150 km, a correction, the entry corridor, splashdown with the crew alive and under a peak g. Along the way: power positive through the eclipses, contact with the ground stations except behind the Moon, and cabin supplies left at splashdown.
- Steps: the batteries and fuel cells, the antenna and the next station pass, the transfer planner to the Moon, Aim Pe for a free return, the correction, pointing the capsule for entry, the parachutes, the sea.

## Done when

- A script with player-level controls completes it.
- The tutorial runs start to finish in the running game.
