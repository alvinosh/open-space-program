# T167: Scenario and tutorial: first launch to orbit

The first ten minutes. A crewed rocket on the pad at the Cape, and a guided flight to a circular orbit that works the first time, for someone who has never played.

## Scope

- Pick the craft (start from `pathfinder_crew`) and tune it by hand until it is forgiving: enough thrust-to-weight, a gravity turn that does not need to be exact, staging that reads on the screen. Not the generator.
- Goals: leave the pad, pass the Kármán line, reach a stable orbit, then circularise.
- Steps: throttle and stage, pitch over, read the navball, SAS, the map view, plan and fly the circularisation burn with the burn executor, warp.
- A beginner profile in the test: a pitch kick that is late or early by 10 seconds still reaches orbit.

## Done when

- A script with player-level controls flies the tutorial to a stable orbit, and so does the beginner profile.
- Someone who has not seen the game finishes it unaided (hand test, noted here).
