# T269: Solar storms

Realistic only. Storms at seeded times, each with a strength and a length, forecast some hours
ahead. During one the dose rises for anything outside a magnetosphere, and links weaken or drop by its
strength. Time warp stops at a forecast and at the start. Casual: off.

## Done when

- A forecast stops warp; the storm then raises the dose of a vessel in lunar orbit and not of one in
  low Earth orbit (test).
- During a storm a link's margin falls by its strength (test).
