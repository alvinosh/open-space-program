# T287: Science and the tech tree: instruments earn science, science unlocks parts, in Casual and Realistic

Asked for on 2026-11-12, ahead of the rest of T260: "I want the tech tree to be laid out, with a clear
progression, obviously different between casual and realistic because of the different parts, the
more science you collect with scientific instruments, the more you can unlock the tech tree".

The loop: fly scientific instruments where they work, get their data home (sent or recovered), earn
science for it, spend it on the tech tree's nodes, and build with what they unlock. Casual has a short
tree for the base game's parts and quick experiments; Realistic has RP-1's tree for the real parts and
experiments that take hours. A new game can still start with every part, as a sandbox (confirmed
2026-11-10, T284).
