# T298: Keys to turn a part in hand on every axis

Said by the user on 2026-11-12: "currently wasd are used to move, but i also need keys to rotate parts".

Today Q and E turn what is in hand about its joint, one step a press; nothing turns it about the other
two axes except the Turn tool's rings after it is on. W, A, S and D move the view.

## Scope

- As KSP: while a part is in hand (or picked), W/S pitch it, A/D yaw it and Q/E roll it, a turn step a
  press (Shift finer, the snap step of T218), about the joint it would go on by. The view keys stop
  moving the view while a part is in hand, as Q and E already do. To confirm with the user: KSP's
  keys, or other keys so WASD always moves the view.
- Space puts it back square. The hint and the controls overlay (T238) say which keys turn.
- Symmetry copies turn with it, mirrored.

## Done when

- A part in hand turns about each axis from the keys and goes on as shown; a builder test for the
  turns, and a capture.
