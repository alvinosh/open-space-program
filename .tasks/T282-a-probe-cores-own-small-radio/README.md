# T282: A probe core's own small radio in Casual

Decided in T250 on 2026-11-10. In Casual a probe core has a small radio of its own that reaches only
near its home planet, so a probe needs an antenna to be flown further out. In Realistic a probe core
has none: without an antenna and a link, an uncrewed probe answers nothing. Today a vessel with no
radio and no crew flies as it always has (T087). A rules entry says which.

## Done when

- In Casual, a probe with only a core is commanded in low Earth orbit and not at the Moon (test).
- In Realistic, the same probe answers nothing anywhere, and does with an antenna in reach of a
  station (test).
- The parts list and the probe core's window say how far its own radio reaches.
