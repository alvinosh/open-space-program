# T313: Upgrades on the space centre buildings

Part of T310. A facility (T294: the launch pad, the builder; and the runway) is upgraded from its
building on the space centre: a click on it shows its level, what it allows, and the next level's
price and limits, with an Upgrade button. Once upgraded the view shows the new level's model (a
bigger pad, a taller assembly building). The editor's panel no longer lists the facilities or
upgrades them; it still refuses a launch over the limits and says by how much.

## Done when

- A capture upgrades the pad from the space centre, the pad's model changes, and the editor's panel
  has no upgrades.
