# T171: Scenario and tutorial: crewed Moon landing

Apollo 11 as a game: land two of the crew near the Sea of Tranquility, take off, meet the command module and come home.

## Scope

- Craft: the Moon stack of T170 with a lander, hand-built.
- Goals: lunar orbit, undock, descend, land within 500 m of the site, ascend, dock, return, splash down.
- Steps: the descent tool, the throttle and the hover, the legs touching down, the ascent, the rendezvous and docking in lunar orbit.

## Done when

- A script with player-level controls completes it.
- The tutorial runs start to finish in the running game.
