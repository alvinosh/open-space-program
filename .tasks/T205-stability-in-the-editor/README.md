# T205: Stability in the editor: centre of mass, of pressure and of thrust

Found on 2026-11-06: the first rocket built from nothing in the editor (a capsule, a short tank and
small fins, at eight g) flew straight for a few seconds and then turned over, because its centre of
pressure was near its centre of mass. The physics was right; the editor gave no way to see it.

## Scope

- Markers for the centres of mass (full and empty), of pressure and of thrust, as KSP's, toggled.
- A check that says when a craft is unstable in the air and by how much (the margin in calibres), from
  the voxel aero the flight uses.

## Done when

- The rocket above shows its centres too close and a warning, and with longer fins the warning goes.
