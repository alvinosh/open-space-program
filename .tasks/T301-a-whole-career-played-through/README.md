# T301: Smoke: a whole career played through, Casual and Realistic

Asked for by the user on 2026-11-12: "At the end, i want a full e2e test of a career mode playthrough,
starting from launching sounding rockets, suborbital test flights, first orbit, moon ect ect, and for
realistic mode, a much slower and realistic game, with much more sounding rockets and unmanned
probes, until there are manned missions ect."

T291 plays a career to its first unlock and upgrade. This one plays it to the Moon and back, in the
smoke crate, each craft built only from what that career has unlocked and within its facilities, the
science and money earned in flight spent between flights as a player would.

## Casual

A sounding rocket to space; suborbital test flights; the first orbit; a crewed orbit; a Moon flyby; a
Moon landing and return. Unlocks and facility upgrades bought between them.

## Realistic

Much slower, as the real programmes went: many sounding rockets (altitude, instruments), then uncrewed
probes to orbit and beyond, and only after those the crewed flights: suborbital, orbit, then the Moon.
The tree, the science rewards and the contracts (T284, T295) have to make that the way to go, not just
allow it: a crewed capsule should not be reachable without the probe stage of the programme.

## Done when

- Both run with no gaps, each flight's goal met, and the report gives per flight: the craft, its
  parts' nodes, the science and money earned, what was unlocked or upgraded after it, and the game
  date; and in total the flights and game time to the Moon in each mode.
- The pacing is checked by the test: Realistic takes clearly more flights, more of them uncrewed,
  before the first crew.
