# T172: Scenario and tutorial: Mars lander

Land a probe on Mars: arrive, aerobrake in the thin air, open a parachute, and finish with retro rockets and legs. Mars needs relief (T110) before this means anything.

## Scope

- Craft: an entry probe with a heat shield, a parachute and a descent stage, built by hand.
- Goals: arrive in the entry corridor, survive the entry, land under a speed on the legs.
- Steps: the entry angle, the parachute timing in thin air, the retro burn.

## Done when

- A script with player-level controls completes it.
- The tutorial runs start to finish in the running game.

## Notes

- A crewed Mars mission is for later: it needs life support (T049), a much larger stack and the time to fly 500 days.
