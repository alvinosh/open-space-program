# T311: The space centre screen

Part of T310. A new or loaded game opens on a view of Cape Canaveral, the game's own terrain at the
launch site, with the pad, the landing strip and the space centre's buildings on it. The camera
circles slowly and can be turned and zoomed within bounds. Pointing at a building outlines it and
names it; a click goes in:

- Vehicle assembly (rockets): the editor as it is for the pad.
- Hangar (planes): the editor for the runway.
- Research, Mission control, Tracking station: T314 and T315; until then they say so.
- The pad and the runway: what was last built, launched from there.

Leaving the editor (Back) or a building's screen comes back here, not to the title screen. The
title screen's Continue and Load come here too.

Buildings are placeholder blocks here (T312 makes the models). Where they stand: a guess, near the
real ones (the VAB west of LC-39A, the SLF to its north-west), but closer together so the view takes
them all in; to show the user.
