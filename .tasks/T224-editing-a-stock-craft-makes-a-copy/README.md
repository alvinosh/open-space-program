# T224: Editing a stock craft makes a copy

The vessels list mixes the craft the game ships with what the player builds. Changing the Saturn V
leaves it unclear whether the Saturn V itself changed.

## Scope

- The first change to a shipped craft makes a copy ("Saturn V (copy)"), kept with the player's
  craft (T020 saves them), listed under a heading of its own above the shipped ones.
- The shipped craft never changes.

## Done when

- Changing a shipped craft and leaving keeps both: the shipped one as it was, the copy as changed.

## Where it comes from

From the editor review of 2026-11-07: the user asked for everything wrong with the editor from a UX point of view, then for each finding to be filed as a task. The epic, T221, lists them all.
