# T147: Overhaul leftovers: craft content

Craft and part content the overhaul left open. Fix the craft files by hand until they fly their mission; do not polish the generator.

## Scope

- Historical craft still fake strap-ons and keep the converter's engine rings (Starship's 33 Raptors among them) until they are rebuilt by hand (was issue 5 of the old list).
- The generator's other craft (Saturn IB's clustered tanks drawn as one, Juno, the sounding rockets) are still converted stages.
- Gemini's OAMS and its fuel cells are mass in the equipment section, not parts (the fuel cells come with the power task).
- The SM's bays hold cylinder tanks, as the real one's do; tanks of other shapes wait for the physics to run on parts.
- The SM's RCS uses the stock hypergolic quad, much stronger than Apollo's.
- Strap-ons have no air rudders or verniers of their own (the R-7 family).
- The Mercury-Atlas is a stage and a half: the sustainer holds all 113 t and burns from the start.
- The N1's Blok V tapers to the L3 instead of a flat shoulder.
- Capsules keep their flat docking-hatch tops (the docking ports task); the escape towers are not built (the launch escape task).
