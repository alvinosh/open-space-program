# T266: Living space, comfort and stress

Realistic only. Room per person, comforts (a window, exercise, a crewmate to talk to, a link home,
gravity from the ground or a spinning part at a = ω²r), and how long they have been out. Stress grows
while they are short of these and falls when not. A stressed person makes mistakes (shuts something
off, vents a little of a resource, deletes data) and in the end cannot work. Casual: off.

## Done when

- Three people in a capsule for a month break down; in a station with room and comforts they do not
  (test).
- A habitat part spun up counts as gravity by ω²r (test).
