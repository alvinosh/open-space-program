# T225: A click on a part tile does one thing

A click on a tile puts the part straight on when the craft is empty, and otherwise takes it in hand
to be put on a ring. The same click does two different things.

## Scope

- Either the first part is also taken in hand and put down with a click in the hangar, or the hint
  says before the click what it will do. Decide which when building it.

## Done when

- A new player can tell from the screen what a click on a tile will do, empty craft or not.

## Where it comes from

From the editor review of 2026-11-07: the user asked for everything wrong with the editor from a UX point of view, then for each finding to be filed as a task. The epic, T221, lists them all.
