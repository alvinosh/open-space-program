# T168: Scenario and tutorial: rendezvous

The `rendezvous` scenario is practice with no end. Make it a mission with a tutorial: a crewed ship, a station on a slightly different orbit, and the steps to meet it.

## Scope

- Craft: a ship with enough RCS and delta-v to do it twice over, built by hand to be pleasant to fly.
- Goals: match planes, bring the closest approach under 1 km, then end up within 100 m of the station at under 0.5 m/s relative speed and hold that for 30 s. When docking ports exist (T040) a version ends in a docking.
- Steps: target the station, the target window, the relative velocity on the navball, a phasing orbit, the closest-approach markers, the final approach with RCS.
- The rendezvous panel and the navball target frames (T083, T081) make it nicer; neither is needed to start.

## Done when

- A script with player-level controls completes it (a `cargo smoke` mission).
- The tutorial runs start to finish in the running game.
