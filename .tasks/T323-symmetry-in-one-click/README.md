# T323: Pick the symmetry count in one click, near where the player is working

From the `first_rocket` playtest's figures (2026-11-16): four fins take three clicks on the symmetry
button, which is in the bottom right corner, 1,237 px from the parts list. That first pointing is the
hardest in the whole build (5.3 bits); with the three clicks it is 5.5 s of a person's 35 s to build.
Also: the part settings' number boxes are 22 px tall, under the 24 px a target should be.

## Done when

- The count is picked in one click (a short row 1 2 3 4 6 8, or a menu from the button), or with the
  part in hand, and `first_rocket` needs fewer inputs and less person time to build.
- Number boxes are at least 24 px tall.
