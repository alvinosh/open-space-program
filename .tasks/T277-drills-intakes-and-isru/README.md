# T277: Drills, intakes and making propellant where you land

A drill that works landed and an intake that works in an atmosphere, each making what T276 says is
there at a rate from how much there is and the power it gets, and converters as processes (T262). In
the parts list.

Casual: a drill makes ore and one converter makes liquid fuel and oxidiser. Realistic: ice to water,
electrolysis, Sabatier, oxygen from Martian CO2.

## Done when

- A lander at the Moon's pole refills its oxidiser (from ore in Casual, from ice in Realistic) (test).
- Two drills on one place each make their share, and stop when storage is full (test).
