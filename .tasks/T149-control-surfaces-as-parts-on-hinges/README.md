# T149: Control surfaces as parts on hinges

A control surface is still part of its lifting surface and flies the flap model; it is cut out of the wing's loft along the hinge and turns on a pivot. Make it a separate lofted part on an actuated hinge joint, driven by the pitch, yaw and roll commands.

## Done when

- A wing's flap is a part of its own on a hinge, placed in the editor, and flies as before (the golden figures do not move).
