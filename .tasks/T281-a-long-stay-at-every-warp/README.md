# T281: A long stay at every warp, saved halfway

A crewed station for 180 days, under Casual and under Realistic, flown three ways: a day at real time
then warp, the highest warp throughout, and saved and loaded halfway. All three end with the same
supplies, health, dose, data and failures. This is the spec's acceptance list (§16) in one run.

## Done when

- The three runs agree under both modes, as a smoke flight or a sim test, and the report says what
  each system did.
