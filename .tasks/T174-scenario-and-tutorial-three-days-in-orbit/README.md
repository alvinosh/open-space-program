# T174: Scenario and tutorial: three days in orbit

The systems tutorial. A crewed ship in low Earth orbit that has to be kept alive for three days, so the player learns the parts the Moon trip depends on before it depends on them.

## Scope

- Craft: a small crewed ship built by hand with batteries, a solar array, a fuel cell, an antenna and a cabin with consumables.
- Goals: power never at zero through the eclipses, contact on every ground-station pass that is long enough, cabin oxygen and water above a limit at the end, a loss-free crew.
- Steps: the power readout and the eclipse timeline, pointing the array, the load priorities, the comms window and the next pass, the consumables and the cabin limits, warping to the next event with the warp stopping at signal loss.

## Done when

- A script with player-level controls completes it, and a variant that ignores the arrays fails at the first eclipse.
- The tutorial runs start to finish in the running game.

## Where it comes from

- Planning of 2026-11-01: batteries, antennas and life support are core to the game, so they get a scenario of their own.
