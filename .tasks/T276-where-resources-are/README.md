# T276: Where resources are, on every body

Each body record can say what is in its ground and its air, and where: water ice near the poles and in
shaded craters, regolith on rocky bodies, gases from the atmosphere's makeup. A query gives what is at
a place. A mod adds deposits with data only.

Casual: ore anywhere on solid ground, at one level. Realistic: the real spread.

## Done when

- The Moon's poles give more water ice than its equator (test).
- A deposit added by the example mod shows up with no code.
